Mission

A lack of direction comes from a lack of understanding.

To move forward, as a democracy, Pakistan must study itself: its society, its institutions and its values. Then recognize its failures, realize its responsibilities, and build its ideals.

What we do

We study Pakistan through what we are told: the news, the bulletins, the dramas, the textbooks, the magazines, the debates in parliament, and anything else that shapes what we come to believe.

Collect

We gather what the country says and keep it: broadcasts, newspapers, textbooks, magazines, the record of parliament. The archives are published so anyone can work from them.

Data→ Describe

Then we say plainly what the record shows: who says what, what spreads, what is left out, and how all of it shifts over time. The methods are published beside the numbers.

Reports→ Argue

Description is where we start, not where we stop. With a record in hand we can argue about what Pakistan's information should be, and what we owe each other inside it.

Arguments→

Why

Daily, 240 million Pakistanis consume hours of information from news channels, TV, social media, YouTube, the internet. The information we consume shapes how we think, what we believe in, and how we act. Pakistan's information ecosystem is rapidly evolving and is severely understudied, and any understanding we have is borrowed from mismatched Western metrics and frameworks.

For example.

What are the news television anchors saying?

How are they framing news, favourable or against the establishment?

Do the news media outlets present vastly different information?

What are people saying on social media platforms?

How did Pakistan do discourse on important issues before digital media?

These questions remain unanswered not because there is no interest, but because the intellectual and data infrastructure that collects and examines at large scales the state of Pakistan's information culture does not exist.

We, at semicolon, work towards understanding the information ecosystem of Pakistan. How individual citizens consume, exhibit and adopt beliefs, through modern information systems and without them.

Goal

The goal is to study ourselves with knowledge made from within, and to argue from it a better future.

Join us
